Card Counting

How counting works (Hi-Lo)

Low cards left in the shoe help the dealer; high cards help you. Counting keeps a rough score of which kind is left. Give every card you see a value and keep a running total in your head, called the running count.

2 3 4 5 6+1
7 8 90
10 J Q K A−1

Start at 0 when the deck is shuffled. A high positive count means lots of tens and aces are still to come, which is good for you, so you bet more. A full deck adds up to exactly 0.

Tip: cards that cancel out (a 5 and a K, say) add 0, so count them in pairs rather than one by one.

Deck Countdown

Turn through a shuffled deck as fast as you can while keeping the count. A full deck always ends at 0, so a few cards are held back unseen. Your final count has to be right for the time to count.
